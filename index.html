Full Disclosed Ayurvedic Formulation

Every batch is prepared with slow manual maceration in pure cold-pressed sesame and coconut oil bases:

  • 1. Bhringraj (Eclipta alba): King of hair vitality and root anchorage.
  • 2. Amla (Phyllanthus emblica): Vitamin C natural shield against damage.
  • 3. Shikakai (Acacia concinna): Natural surfactant for mild cleansing.
  • 4. Reetha (Sapindus mukorossi): Pure botanical saponin soapnut.
  • 5. Rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis): Aromatic follicular stimulant.
  • 6. Jatamansi (Nardostachys jatamansi): Root strengthener and scalp calmer.
  • 7. Neem (Azadirachta indica): Anti-microbial dandruff protection.
  • 8. Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri): Scalp cooling and texture conditioner.
  • 9. Methi (Fenugreek): Protein & nicotinic acid strand repair.
  • 10. Curry Leaves (Murraya koenigii): Natural pigment and luster preserver.
  • 11. Vetiver (Khus): Cooling herbal aromatic balance.
